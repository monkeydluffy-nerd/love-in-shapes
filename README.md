# Love, in shapes

A live, ever-moving showcase of love symbols. About 1,500 glowing particles flow along a shape, then dissolve and reform as the next one. It's a single web page with no frameworks and no dependencies.

**Live demo:** https://monkeydluffy-nerd.github.io/love-in-shapes

## The symbols

The heart, infinity, two rings, the rose, Cupid's arrow, the moon and star, and two hearts. Each one is drawn from a math curve, and each has its own color mood that follows your light or dark setting.

## How to use it

- Watch it. It morphs to a new symbol every 8 seconds.
- Move your cursor through the particles and they part around it.
- Click or tap anywhere to scatter them.
- **Show me another** jumps to a random symbol. The arrow keys step through them in order.
- **Pause** stops the auto-morphing. If your device has "reduce motion" on, the page stays calm by default.

## Built with

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in one file (`index.html`), drawn live on an HTML canvas. Fonts are Caprasimo and Figtree from Google Fonts.

## Run it locally

Download `index.html` and open it in any browser. There is nothing to install.
